HOW TO PLAY

Controls

SPACE Jump — leap over low obstacles
S Duck — slide under high obstacles
D Kick — smash through breakable walls

Obstacle Colors

Green glow = Jump
Blue glow = Duck
Red glow = Kick

Game Modes

Story Mode: Select a map and difficulty. Reach the finish line to earn XP. Wrong action or no action = death!

Endless Mode: Run forever! Speed keeps increasing. Score = distance. Beat your high score!

Tips

You are ALWAYS running — you cannot stop!

If you miss an obstacle, you die. React fast!

Earn XP to unlock new characters in the Shop.
